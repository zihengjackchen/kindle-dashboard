# Asset provenance

- `live-*.png`: real device capture and running NAS editor/renderer screenshots,
  with capture dates and privacy edits documented in `docs/showcase/live/README.md`.
  Casing graphics are code-drawn illustrations, not photographs.
- Other dashboard PNGs retained from the initial composition are synthetic samples;
  the revised composition references only the `live-*.png` files.
- `paper-notes.wav`: original synthesized notes and clicks generated
  by `scripts/build-brag-assets.py`; project MIT license. No sampled recordings.
- `gsap.min.js`: GSAP 3.14.2, Standard GSAP License;
  original license header retained. https://gsap.com/standard-license/
- No proprietary system fonts are redistributed. Video uses generic sans-serif.
- Brag's bundled music is excluded because its redistribution license is undocumented.
